# Poetics entry audit — research batches, 13 October 2026

341 directory records; 534 source-linked relationships. 165 records have a scoped review in this audit layer; 176 remain pending a full claim-by-claim re-audit. Earlier provenance remains intact. These counts do not mean every field of a reviewed record is independently verified.

## Workflow

1. Resolve identity and aliases without merging people from similar names.
2. Read sources; separate event, announcement and source publication dates.
3. Record each supported claim, source URL, locator, evidence type and review date.
4. Extract named relationships with their exact role: organiser, announced performer, actual performer, judge, reported winner, editor, publisher or venue. A tag alone is not a role; sharing a city is not participation.
5. Resolve existing IDs before adding a record; connect new endpoints reciprocally.
6. Verify current activity separately. Evidence expires after 12 months; silence is not closure.
7. Follow confirmed endpoints into their next sources; queue unresolved leads.

verification-queue.json, generated by scripts/verification-queue.cjs, covers every entry and its next research step. No social crawler or scheduled re-audit is deployed.
